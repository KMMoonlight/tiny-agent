# Meeting Notes Skill

When producing meeting notes, follow these rules:

## Structure

The notes must contain exactly these four sections, in this order:

1. **会议主题** — one sentence
2. **关键结论** — decisions reached, one bullet each; distinguish "已确定" from "待确认"
3. **行动项** — a list of "负责人 → 事项 → 时间点"; if the owner or deadline
   is unknown, write "待定" explicitly instead of guessing
4. **遗留问题** — open questions raised but not resolved

## Style

- Facts only: record what the material says, never add interpretation
  or advice of your own
- Quote numbers and proper nouns exactly as they appear in the source
- Keep the whole notes under 300 Chinese characters
